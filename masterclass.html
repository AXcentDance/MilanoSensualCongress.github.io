The details / 2026 reference

Good to know.

The answers below describe the 2026 edition. The 2027 programme will be announced separately.

Is this Bachata masterclass for advanced dancers?

Yes. It is designed for dancers with Bachata foundations who want advanced training in technique, musicality, connection, body mechanics and social dance quality.

What do advanced Bachata workshops focus on?

The workshops focus on refined movement, lead-follow communication, musical interpretation, body control, connection and tools that apply directly to social dancing and performance.

Do I need a Full Pass for the masterclasses?

Yes. A valid Full Pass is required before adding the Masterclass Upgrade. The masterclass cannot be attended with the upgrade alone.
