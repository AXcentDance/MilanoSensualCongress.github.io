International Jack & Jill / The 2026 competition story

The competition dossier / 2026 reference

Meet the moment.
Make it yours.

The International Jack & Jill brings dancers from across Europe together on the Milano stage. It is a chance to put your social dancing into focus, with connection, musicality and technique at the centre.

The 2026 jury is composed of international Bachata masters. The competition offers dancers a platform to be seen by experienced artists, share their dancing and take the next step in their development.
