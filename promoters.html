How to get involved

Start with a conversation.

  1. 01

    Introduce yourself

    Tell us where you’re based, how you connect with dancers and why you would like to work with Milano Sensual Congress.

  2. 02

    Explore the possibilities

    Discuss your ideas and the proposed collaboration with our team. Any responsibilities, promotional arrangements and benefits are agreed directly.

  3. 03

    Bring your people along

    Once the collaboration is confirmed, share the agreed information with your community and help them prepare for their Milano experience.

A useful first message

Tell us about
your corner of Bachata.

Contact the team ↗

Collaboration details are discussed individually. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a promoter agreement.

  • You & your city

    Your name, city and country, plus your school, team or community if you represent one.

  • Where you connect

    Your social profiles, website or the events and classes through which you reach dancers.

  • Your community

    The people you dance with, the languages you communicate in and any group you would like to bring.

  • Your ideas

    Relevant experience and how you would like to introduce Milano Sensual Congress to your community.

Before you get in touch

A few things to know.

Can I get involved from another country?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from promoters and dance communities across Europe and worldwide. Tell us where you are based and which community you would like to connect with the congress.

Do I need to run a dance school?

No. Teachers, schools, event organisers, creators and active members of the Bachata community can all introduce themselves and propose a collaboration.

What are the rewards or commission terms?

Promoter conditions for 2027 are not published here. Discuss any benefits, commissions, discounts or promotional arrangements directly with our team before starting a collaboration.

Are the 2027 event dates confirmed?

Dates for the 2027 edition are still to be announced. The booking details and event information labelled as 2026 elsewhere on this preview are reference information for that edition.
