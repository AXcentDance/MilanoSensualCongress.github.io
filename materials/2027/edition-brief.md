## A congress for every style and level

On 28 September 2026, the owner confirmed three workshop rooms and the priority
of building a congress for beginner, intermediate and advanced dancers, with
masterclasses also part of the programme. On **29 September 2026**, the owner
clarified the planning aim: work toward **three different styles and three
different levels each hour** across the **three confirmed workshop rooms**, to
help dancers find an option suited to their level. The hourly coverage is an
effort and aim, not a guarantee for every hour or every dancer. The final
timetable, room assignments, complete artist lineup and masterclass booking terms are not yet
announced. Do not imply that one room is permanently assigned to each style or
level, or that masterclasses are included in every pass.

The owner also confirmed Bachata Sensual, Bachazouk, Esencia Style and Bachata
Dominicana on 28 September 2026. These are confirmed workshop styles; their
placement in any illustrative timetable does not establish times, rooms or
level assignments in the final programme. The illustration's 19:00, 20:00,
21:00 and 22:00 labels remain examples, not confirmed programme times.

On 29 September 2026, the owner requested a more compact vertical layout and
a single red **Masterclass** block at the illustrative 22:00 row. It spans the
three room columns, with no parallel workshops shown in that example row.
This is a presentation example; the final timetable and actual room assignments
remain unannounced. It does not establish a confirmed 22:00 masterclass or pass
inclusion. The owner subsequently removed the visible example note from the
homepage; its table's accessible label still identifies the illustration and
the forthcoming final 2027 schedule.

English: **A congress for every style and level.** Across three workshop rooms,
we aim to offer three different styles and three levels each hour, helping
dancers find a workshop suited to their level. Explore Bachata Sensual,
Bachazouk, Esencia Style and Bachata Dominicana, with beginner, intermediate
and advanced options. Masterclasses offer another way to deepen your learning.

Italiano: **Un congresso per ogni stile e livello.** In tre sale workshop,
lavoriamo per proporre tre stili diversi e tre livelli ogni ora, per aiutarti
a trovare una lezione adatta al tuo livello. Esplora Bachata Sensual, Bachazouk,
Esencia Style e Bachata Dominicana, con proposte per principianti, intermedi
e avanzati. Le masterclass sono un'altra occasione per approfondire.

## Creative direction and assets

The homepage and flagship Duomo film use a cinematic emerald, warm ivory,
Italian red and restrained architectural gold treatment. The event title remains
fixed over softly moving tricolour smoke and sky. Follow the
[homepage palette](../../css/home-premiere.css) and the
[canonical brand rules](../../.agent/rules/brand-coherence.md). Other page
families retain their individual compositions and the broader palette in
[edition-personality.css](../../css/edition-personality.css). Each promotional
piece should follow its corresponding subject reference in the asset register;
do not maintain a second palette specification that drifts from these sources.

On 28 September 2026, the owner requested the
[official wordmark film v07](sources/frecce-tricolori-duomo/README.md#official-wordmark-v07--without-the-year)
for both local homepages: the existing pink/purple and white script logo over
the same Duomo and moving tricolour footage, with the separately composited
gold year removed. The film fills the viewport horizontally at its natural
16:9 ratio. This refines the v06 film selected on 27 September; v06 and all
earlier sources and exports remain preserved. The event year still appears
in the surrounding date information. These local changes are unpublished.

Use heavy Inter for oversized festival headlines, with Playfair Display for
selective italic accents and Inter for body text, following the
[existing font setup](../../css/fonts.css). Keep copy short and direct. The
owner refined the direction toward a more expressive, animated dance-festival
identity on 26 September 2026, then explicitly requested richer colour, light,
and a distinct personality for every page. Repeated horizontal bands and the
monochrome corporate treatment are retired. Keep the official logo's original
artwork, colours, lettering, and proportions even when the surrounding design
uses the 2027 palette. The
[official logo notes](../../images/brand/milano-sensual-congress-official-logo-notes.md)
identify the approved master and the build process.

The [asset register](asset-register.md) identifies the existing logo, Duomo
visual, main film, hotel photography, and artist media in place. Web-resolution
assets are not automatically suitable for large-format print. Record a suitable
source and its permitted use before producing a new print or advertising export.

New materials belong in the edition folders described in the
[materials catalog](../README.md#filing-new-work). Add each source and export to
the register so another collaborator can locate the correct version quickly.
