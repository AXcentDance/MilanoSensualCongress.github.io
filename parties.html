The details / 2026 reference

Good to know.

The answers below describe the 2026 edition. The 2027 programme will be announced separately.

What time are the bachata parties at Milano Sensual Congress 2026?

The official social dance times are: Friday social dance 23:00–05:00, Saturday afternoon social 16:00–20:00, Saturday social dance 23:00–05:00, and Sunday afternoon social 16:00–22:00.

How many hours of social dancing are there?

Milano Sensual Congress 2026 offers 20+ hours of official social dancing across the weekend: two night parties until 5 AM and two afternoon socials, all inside Devero Hotel.

Which ticket do I need to access the parties?

The Full Pass includes all parties and afternoon socials. To access the Saturday afternoon social you need either a Full Pass or a Saturday Day Pass. The Full Social Pass costs €75 and includes Friday night, Saturday night and Sunday afternoon socials.

Where do the parties take place?

All parties and socials take place at Devero Hotel in Cavenago di Brianza, the official congress venue. Workshops, parties and accommodation are under one roof, so you can dance until sunrise and sleep on site.
