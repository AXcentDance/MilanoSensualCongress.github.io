When will the Bachata workshop schedule be announced?

The complete Milano Sensual Congress 2026 workshop schedule, including class-by-class times, rooms and artist assignments, will be published in November.

What time are the Bachata workshops and parties?

The current preview has workshops from 18:00 to 23:00 on Friday, 10:00 to 20:00 on Saturday and 10:00 to 16:00 on Sunday. The official social dance times are 23:00 to 05:00 on Friday, 16:00 to 20:00 and 23:00 to 05:00 on Saturday, and 16:00 to 22:00 on Sunday. See the full details on the parties & social dancing page.

Can the congress program change?

Yes. This preview is subject to slight changes while the final Bachata congress program and artist timetable are confirmed.
