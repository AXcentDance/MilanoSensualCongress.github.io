Festival Calendar

Bachata Festivals
in Europe 2026-2027

The month-by-month calendar of the European Bachata season — every major congress and festival, from Milan to the Spanish coast.

Home / News / Bachata Festivals in Europe Calendar

Planning a season of Bachata festivals in Europe is half the fun of dancing it. From an executive hotel takeover in Milan to beach parties on the Spanish coast, the 2026-2027 season is stacked. Here is the full calendar of the Bachata events worth building your year around, month by month.

Whether you are chasing your first international weekend or you already collect festival wristbands, this guide covers the key Bachata congresses and festivals across Italy, Spain, Romania and beyond — with what makes each one different, so you can pick the events that match your style, level and budget. New to the format? Start with our guide on what to expect at a Bachata congress.

November 2026
Milano Sensual Congress — Milan, Italy

The European winter season opens in style. On 20-22 November 2026 the Milano Sensual Congress takes over the four-star Devero Hotel & Spa just outside Milan: every workshop, social and show under one roof, with your room an elevator ride away from the dance floor. The line-up of international Bachata masters is strictly curated — Gero y Migle, Klau y Ros, Cristian y Gabriella, David y Ines and more — and the weekend includes an international Jack & Jill plus socials that genuinely run until sunrise.

It is the all-in-one concept that sets Milan apart: no taxis between venues, a spa for your tired feet, and a crowd that flies in from all over Europe. Check the full workshop program, book the hotel packages early — they sell out — and secure your Full Pass before the next price increase. Arriving by plane? The official transfer service connects all three Milan airports to the venue.

January 2027
Bachata Sensual World Congress — Huelva, Spain

Deep winter belongs to Andalusia. In mid-January the Bachata Sensual World Congress (BSWC) gathers the global Sensual community in Huelva for close to a week of immersion in the style, organised around the dancers who shaped it. If Bachata Sensual is your language, this is its capital — expect technique at the source, world-championship energy and a genuinely international floor.

Also in January: Wild Divas — Rome, Italy

Italy answers with something unique: Wild Divas in Rome, the all-female festival dedicated entirely to Bachata Lady Style. If styling, followers' technique and a supportive, high-energy environment are what you are after, it is a beautiful way to start the year — and a perfect warm-up weekend between the winter congresses.

February 2027
Sensual Bachata Festival — Bucharest, Romania

Eastern Europe's scene has exploded, and the Sensual Bachata Festival in Bucharest is its proof. Reaching its fourth edition in mid-February, it brings a big international line-up to a modern events hall in the Romanian capital, with the warm, hungry-to-dance crowd that makes emerging scenes so addictive. Flights to Bucharest are cheap from most of Europe — a smart pick for dancers watching their budget.

April 2027
Fever Bachata World Meeting — Gandia, Spain

Spring means the coast. From 22 to 26 April 2027 the Fever Bachata World Meeting lands at the Hotel Gandia Palace in Gandia, València — four days and nights with over 60 hours of socials, pool and beach parties, an international Jack & Jill and the Dance Game World Cup with more than €10,000 in prizes. It is the closest thing Bachata has to a summer-holiday festival, with sea air included.

Dates TBA
Bachata Day — Milan, Italy

Keep an eye on Milan year-round: Bachata Day is the city's festival-format event dedicated to Bachata, and a great way to sample the Milanese scene between the big congress weekends. Dates are announced seasonally — follow the scene or check back on our news page, where we track everything happening in the Italian Bachata scene.

How to Choose Your Festival Season

You do not need all of them — you need the right ones. Our advice for building a season: anchor it with one all-in-one hotel congress where you fully immerse (that is Milan in November), add one style-specific event that matches your focus (BSWC for Sensual purists, Wild Divas for lady style), and one travel adventure in a scene you have never danced in (Bucharest is the value pick; Gandia the beach pick). For a deeper comparison of the continent's biggest events, read our Top 5 Bachata congresses in Europe guide.

Booking tips that apply to every festival on this list: full passes are cheapest months ahead and never get cheaper; hotel-venue rooms sell out before tickets do; and if you are travelling without a partner, don't hesitate — congresses are built for it (here is our full solo dancer's guide).
