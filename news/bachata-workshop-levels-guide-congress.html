Three rooms. A place for your bachata.

The workshop format is simple: three rooms run in parallel during each scheduled workshop hour. Room 1 welcomes beginners and all levels, Room 2 is intermediate, and Room 3 is advanced. You can plan your learning around where you are today.

01

Beginner / open level

Room 1 · Your first year and beyond

Yes, beginners belong here. Room 1 is truly beginner-friendly, including dancers with less than one year of experience. You do not need to reach an intermediate level before joining the congress.

Open level also welcomes experienced dancers who want to revisit their foundations. If you are new, returning after a break or building confidence, this is a welcoming place to start.

02

Intermediate level

Room 2 · Build on your foundations

Comfortable with your basic step and already enjoying social dancing? Room 2 gives intermediate dancers a place in the programme, alongside the beginner and advanced rooms.

Choose this level when you can follow a class while keeping your timing and connection. Look for ideas you can bring to your next social dance, and give yourself time to practise them.

03

Advanced level

Room 3 · Go deeper into your dancing

Experienced dancers have their own room too. Room 3 is the advanced track in the regular workshop programme, for dancers ready to work at a more demanding level.

A congress for everyone includes space for your next challenge. Explore the international Bachata artist lineup and use the programme to choose the sessions that interest you.

The three-room format applies to scheduled workshop hours. Check the official Bachata workshop programme for class times and artists. Optional masterclasses are a separate upgrade requiring a valid Full Pass.

You do not have to be advanced to belong.

It is easy to watch a beautiful congress video and think, “I will go when I am that good.” But the dancers you see all had a first class, a first party and a first invitation to dance. Your first Bachata Congress can be part of learning, too.

At Milano Sensual Congress, the beginner / open-level room makes that invitation concrete. You are not left waiting for the one class that might suit you: the three-room format gives every level a workshop option during each scheduled workshop hour.

You do not need to become a different dancer before coming. Come as the dancer you are.

If you are comparing beginner-friendly Bachata Festivals in Italy, look beyond the highlight videos. A useful question is whether the programme has a place for your current experience. Here, Room 1 is part of the plan from the start.

Choose a room that helps you enjoy learning.

Start with your experience and how comfortable you feel in class. The first-year guidance for Room 1 is a welcome for newer dancers, not a deadline for moving up. People learn at different speeds, and open-level workshops can be valuable at any stage.

  1. Begin where you feel comfortable. If you are new or unsure, the beginner / open-level room is a good starting point.
  2. Balance challenge with understanding. Choose a class where you can follow the explanation and practise with control.
  3. Leave room to enjoy the weekend. Take breaks, keep a few ideas to revisit and use the social floor to practise familiar material.

Travelling with friends who dance at different levels? You can each choose a suitable room during the same workshop hour, then meet again afterwards. You can share the congress without needing identical experience.

A Bachata Festival weekend goes beyond the classroom.

Workshops give you ideas; social dancing gives you a reason to use them. The Bachata parties and socials bring dancers together around the music, whether your favourite dance uses simple basics or years of experience.

The international Bachata artists at Milano Sensual Congress offer different influences to explore. Let curiosity guide you: connection, rhythm, musicality and movement can keep your dancing interesting long after the weekend.

Coming on your own is an option too. Read our guide to attending a Bachata Congress alone for practical ways to settle in and meet people. A smile, an introduction and a respectful invitation to dance are good places to begin. Our social dancing and workshop etiquette guide will help you feel prepared.

FAQ

Your workshop questions, answered.

Is Milano Sensual Congress suitable for beginners?

Yes. Room 1 is beginner-friendly and open to all levels, including dancers with less than one year of experience. You do not need to be intermediate or advanced to attend.

Is there a workshop for my level every hour?

During each scheduled workshop hour, classes run in three rooms: Room 1 beginner / open level, Room 2 intermediate and Room 3 advanced. This means each level has a suitable option; it does not mean workshops run continuously throughout the weekend.

What does open level mean at this congress?

Room 1 welcomes beginners and dancers of all levels. Newer dancers can start here, while experienced dancers can choose open-level classes to revisit their foundations.

Do I need a dance partner to attend?

You can attend the congress alone. Partner rotation is common in congress workshops; follow each teacher’s instructions for the particular class. You can also use the solo dancer guide to prepare for your weekend.

Are advanced workshops and masterclasses the same thing?

No. Room 3 is the advanced room in the regular workshop programme. The optional masterclasses are a separate upgrade and require a valid Full Pass. Check the ticket and masterclass pages for full details.

When and where is Milano Sensual Congress 2026?

The congress takes place from 20 to 22 November 2026 at Devero Hotel, Largo Kennedy 1, Cavenago di Brianza, in the Milan area, Italy.
